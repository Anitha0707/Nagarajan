Which LIC plans can you help me choose?

I guide families on LIC term plans, endowment and money-back plans, child plans, pension and annuity plans, unit-linked plans and the newer health plans. Browse the LIC Plans section, then ask for a recommendation based on your age, goals and budget.

How do I choose between term, endowment and pension plans?

It depends on your goal. Term plans give pure protection at a low cost, endowment and money-back plans add savings, and pension or annuity plans create income for retirement. Share your age, dependants and goals and I will explain suitable options in plain language.

Do you help with premium renewals, policy servicing and claims?

Yes. I help with renewal reminders and payments, reviving lapsed policies, nominee and address changes, loan requests, and the documents needed for maturity, survival and death claims.

Which insurance companies do you represent?

LIC of India, Star Health, Galaxy Health and United India Insurance, so you can look at life and health cover in one place.
